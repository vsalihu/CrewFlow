Additional tested rules: idempotency replay/conflict, append-only audit and revisions for the runtime role, cross-organisation foreign keys, impact-token tampering, stale draft after amendment, driver manifest without phone numbers.

Journey and site-entrance safety (`journey-safety.test.ts`): a departed trip cannot be revised (record a disruption); removing a passenger reopens their transport need with a task; adding a passenger supersedes manifests already drafted and asks for the driver to reconfirm; cancelled or wrongly booked passengers, a driver whose own shift is elsewhere, and drop-offs at the wrong entrance are refused; an entrance must belong to the line's site, be verified and be valid on the date (readiness, booking drafts, manifests and trip validation share one rule); a site change clears the old entrance; an open source conflict blocks trip publishing, manifests and recording a send of a draft made before the conflict.

Attendance (`attendance.test.ts`, `e2e/attendance.spec.ts`): arrivals and no-shows (a no-show opens a replacement task; a never-confirmed worker cannot be a no-show), the person who entered a timesheet cannot approve it, coordinators and branch managers cannot export, break rules are versioned and set only by an administrator, concurrent edits are version-checked.
